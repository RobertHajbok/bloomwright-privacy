Bloomwright Privacy Policy

Last updated: 30 October 2026

Bloomwright (the "app") is a game published by Deluxe Software Solutions SRL, Romania ("we", "us"). This policy explains what information the app uses and why. You do not need an account to play, and we never ask for your name, email address or phone number inside the app.

What stays on your device

Your garden, your collection, your petals, level, quests, decorations, upgrades and your settings are stored only on your device. If you uninstall the app or clear its data, they are removed. We do not collect them and we do not have a copy.

Information collected by our service providers

The app uses two services. With your consent where the law requires it, they receive technical information from your device, such as an advertising identifier or a random app user identifier, your approximate location derived from your IP address, device model, operating system version, language, and how ads and purchases are used.

ServicePurposeProvider
Google AdMobShowing optional rewarded ads (an ad you choose to watch to skip a wait, double an order's reward or get a few free petals) and measuring them. The app has no other ads and never shows an ad you did not ask for.Google LLC / Google Ireland Ltd
RevenueCatHandling in-app purchases (petal packs, a starter bundle, extra worlds and the Garden Club subscription) and restoring them: it receives an anonymous app user ID, your purchases and device information, through Google Play BillingRevenueCat, Inc.

We do not sell your personal information. Payments are handled by Google Play; we never receive your payment details, only the fact that a purchase was made. The app does not currently send analytics or crash reports to us.

Reminder notifications

If you allow notifications, the app schedules reminders on your device (for example when your flowers are ready to bloom). They are created and shown by your device. Nothing about them is sent to us. You can turn them off in the app's settings or in your Android settings.

Consent and your choices

  • If you are in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom or another region that requires it, the app asks for your consent when it starts. Until you have answered, no ad is requested; ads are not personalised unless you consent.
  • You can change or withdraw your choice later in the app's settings (Privacy choices) where your region requires that option.
  • You can reset or limit your advertising identifier in your Android settings (Settings > Privacy > Ads).
  • Watching an ad is always optional. Nothing in the game is locked behind an ad.

Legal basis (EEA and UK)

Consent for ads and their personalisation (where the law requires consent). Our legitimate interest in keeping the app working and processing your purchases.

Retention

Advertising data is kept by Google for the periods set in its own policies. Purchase records are kept by Google Play and RevenueCat. Apart from that we do not keep any data about you ourselves.

International transfers

Our providers may process data outside your country, including in the United States, under the safeguards they publish (such as the EU-US Data Privacy Framework or standard contractual clauses).

Children

Bloomwright is not directed at children under 13 (or under 16 where local law sets that age). We do not knowingly collect information from children. If you believe a child has used the app, contact us and we will help.

Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct, delete or export your data, to object to or restrict processing, and to complain to your data-protection authority (in Romania, ANSPDCP). Because we do not hold identifying details about you, requests about advertising data usually need the advertising identifier of your device. Contact us using the details below.

Changes

If we change this policy we will update the date above and, for significant changes, tell you in the app.
